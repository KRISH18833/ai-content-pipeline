UPI (Unified Payments Interface) was developed by the National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI) and launched in 2016.
It lets users link bank accounts to a mobile app and pay using a virtual payment address (VPA), a QR code, or a phone number.
Transfers are instant, work 24/7, and move money directly between bank accounts.
Multiple apps such as PhonePe, Google Pay, and Paytm can run on the same UPI network, so users are not locked into one app.
Users approve each payment with a UPI PIN.
